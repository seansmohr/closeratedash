Closes and revenue come from the Production Sheet: each client application, by App Date, is one close for the agent in the Agent column, so an add-on sale in a later month counts as its own close. Booked, held and no-show appointments come from the GoHighLevel calendar, by appointment date, credited to the agent the appointment is assigned to; appointments that haven’t happened yet aren’t counted. Every application on the sheet is a held appointment on its App Date, for the agent in the Agent column: if the client (matched by phone number) had a GoHighLevel appointment that day, that appointment counts as held; if not, an extra held appointment is added. Revenue per close averages each application; revenue per client adds up all of a client’s applications in the selected period. Cancelled policies aren’t closes and are left out of both revenue figures. Show rate is held ÷ booked. A no-show is an appointment marked No Show on the calendar, or whose contact’s Appointment Status is No Show; cancelled and rescheduled appointments aren’t counted as booked.
